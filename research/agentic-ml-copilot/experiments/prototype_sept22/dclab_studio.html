One problem blocks the rest. Cell 1 fits the scaler on all 5,000 rows before the split. Test-set statistics leak into training and inflate every score after it.

!Cell 1 · leakage riskFix first

          
The scaler learns each column's mean and spread from the test rows too.

          
DCLAB-R04 · confirmed precedent EXP-002

          
Confidence97%
Score impact−3 to −8 pts

          

            In plain words: the scaler learns the average and spread of every column. Here it learns them from all 5,000 rows, including the 1,000 test rows. So the model gets a small hint about rows it should never see.


            Real-life example: a student who is told tomorrow's exam average already knows something about the exam.


            The fix: split first, fit the scaler on the training rows only, then use it to transform the test rows.
