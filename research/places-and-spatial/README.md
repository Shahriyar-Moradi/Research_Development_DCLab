# Places: values over a map

**Status:** active
**Owner:** DCLab R&D · **Started:** 2026-10-15

## Research question

How much does a random split flatter a model of values over a map (prices across a city), where nearby places are alike, against a split that keeps whole blocks of places on one side? The answer decides when the places kind splits by blocks and how large they are.

## Why it matters for DCLab

The `places` kind (`dclab_rnd/kinds/places.py`) cites this track: its acceptance on the city-prices replica (campaign `kinds_v1`), the random split's optimism measured on blocks (`pitfalls_by_kind_v1`). Before 16.14 the kind pointed at the driving-maps track, which is about road graphs.

## Prediction contract

For modeling work, write this before looking at any score (rule DCLAB-R01):

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Unit of prediction | one place (a listed flat) |
| Prediction moment | when the place is listed: its location, size, rooms and age known |
| Target and window | the value at the place (the price) |
| Allowed information | the place's own attributes and coordinates |
| Forbidden information | anything set after listing (the sale price itself, the time on market) |
| Primary metric | MAE, on held-out blocks of places |

  "experiment_id": "SPA-001",

  "question": "...",
  "hypothesis": "...",
  "setup_summary": "1-3 sentences: exactly what was compared, with numbers",
  "evidence": {"selection_rule": "...", "...": "..."},
  "claims": [{"claim_id": "SPA-001-C1", "kind": "fact | risk | decision | recommendation",

Results that follow the five standard stages can live under `evidence/campaigns/<track>_v1/results/EXP-*.json`, where the evidence index, the critic gate and the SFT builder pick them up automatically.
