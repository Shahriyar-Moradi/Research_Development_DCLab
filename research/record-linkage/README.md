# Record linkage and duplicate detection

**Status:** active
**Owner:** DCLab R&D · **Started:** 2026-10-19

## Research question

Which records are the same thing (a copied bill and its original, one product in two catalogues, one paper in two
libraries), and how well can pairs be told apart once a record and every record it matches are kept on one side of the
split? The answer decides whether DCLab's matching kind (30.16) links pairs by rule, by one field, or by a model.

## Why it matters for DCLab

It is the owner's pattern 4 (copied bills in retail returns fraud) and a step of pattern 10 (one data foundation: entities
resolved across systems). The kind runs in the engine as its own machine (`dclab_rnd/kinds/matching.py`, the library
`dclab_core/matching.py`), and its evidence is this track.

## Prediction contract

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Unit of prediction | one pair of records (a candidate pair blocking formed) |
| Prediction moment | when two records are compared: after both exist |
| Target and window | whether the pair is a known match (the matches table) |
| Allowed information | the fields the person names, of the two records |
| Forbidden information | the matches table itself as a feature, a record's id, any record of another part |
| Primary metric | pair F1 at the threshold chosen on the validation records; precision, recall, average precision and blocking's recall beside it |

## Result schema

Write results in the shape the rest of the repository already understands, so `dclab_rnd.evidence_index` can index them and the SFT builder can learn from them:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "campaign_id": "record_linkage_v1",
  "experiment_id": "RLK-001",
  "dataset": "dataset_key",
  "kind": "data_understanding | leakage_audit | feature_engineering | model_selection | optimization_reliability",
  "task_type": "binary | multiclass | regression | timeseries_regression | graph_node_classification | ...",
  "primary_metric": "roc_auc | average_precision | macro_f1 | mae | ...",
  "question": "...",
  "hypothesis": "...",
  "setup_summary": "1-3 sentences: exactly what was compared, with numbers",
  "evidence": {"selection_rule": "...", "...": "..."},
  "claims": [{"claim_id": "RLK-001-C1", "kind": "fact | risk | decision | recommendation",
              "statement": "...", "evidence": ["evidence.<key>"], "limitations": ["..."]}],
  "provenance": "from dclab_rnd.provenance.capture_provenance(...)"
}
```

Results that follow the five standard stages can live under `evidence/campaigns/<track>_v1/results/EXP-*.json`, where the evidence index, the critic gate and the SFT builder pick them up automatically.

## Conclusions

Write what was learned, with the numbers and the result files that support it. Turn durable lessons into rules in `evidence/knowledge/model_building_rules.jsonl` only after they hold on more than one dataset.
