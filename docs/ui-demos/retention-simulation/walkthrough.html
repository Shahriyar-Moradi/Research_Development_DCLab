    How the simulation works

    The target is non-renewal by the contract due date. A logistic regression uses two inputs available seven days beforehand: days since last use and previous support tickets. It trains on 900 synthetic contracts. On the next 300, its probability estimates are compared with the historical non-renewal rate. The campaign uses 120 separate customers. Fixed random draws let you compare budgets under the same assumptions. The simulator assumes an offer reduces each recipient’s underlying non-renewal risk by 24%. Any renewal difference you see comes from that assumption and chance.
