Judgment benchmark

Does DCLab judge well on work it has never seen?

Without this page, "DCLab is right" is only a claim. With it, the claim has a number. Unseen notebooks and projects carry planted traps, and every policy is scored on the same cases: the standard plan, general LLMs, our own policy model, and human reviewers.
