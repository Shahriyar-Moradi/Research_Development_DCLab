How the gate works

${chip:DCLAB-R20}

1. Deterministic code runs the experiment and writes the numbers.

2. An LLM critic reads the record and challenges each claim.

3. The gate checks every challenge against the recorded numbers. A challenge that misreads them is dropped; the rest stay attached to the record.

4. The record enters the index with its surviving challenges as caveats.

So far: 147 challenges kept, 10 dropped as contradicted by the numbers.

A dropped challenge

${chip:EXP-008}

“The selection rule is not satisfied because the selected stage mean ROC-AUC is 0.7029, not within 0.002 of the best stage mean 0.7172.”

Gate: contradicted. The chosen recipe is ratios at 0.7172, the best mean. The 0.7029 belongs to a different recipe that happens to be called selected.

Why it matters: without the gate, a confident but wrong critique would have become a "lesson" in the training data.
