What happens next

  1. 0
    Data. Upload a table, or start from a dataset the R&D already studied.
  2. 1
    Prediction contract. Say when you predict and what is unknown then. The agent proposes what to forbid.
  3. 2
    Understand the data. The holdout is locked first; the training rows are profiled.
  4. 3
    Audit leakage. Measured: how much the forbidden columns would have inflated the score.
  5. 4
    Feature ladder. Recipes compared on identical folds; the smallest within tolerance wins.
  6. 5
    Algorithm screen. Five families, ranked with fold spread counted.
  7. 6
    Tune and confirm. A few explicit candidates, then the holdout, once.
  8. 7
    Report and notebook. Every decision with its proof, and runnable scikit-learn code.
